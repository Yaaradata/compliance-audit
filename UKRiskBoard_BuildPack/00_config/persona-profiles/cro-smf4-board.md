# Persona Profile -- Chief Risk Officer (SMF4) at board altitude

> Onboarded at Phase 0 on 27 October 2026 from the Stage 1 merged corpus. Grounding IDs are cited inline.
> - **KPI vocabulary:** taken from the research, which comes from firm disclosures and regulator texts. It is not invented.
> - **Daily job and cadence:** practitioner-derived, marked `[unverified -- confirm in CRO interview]` where no source supports them.

## Identity and mandate
PRIMARY_PERSONA: Chief Risk Officer, SMF4. Owns the risk management framework and the risk appetite framework. Responsible for presenting the risk profile against appetite to the Board Risk Committee and the board.

TITLE_VARIANTS: Chief Risk Officer; Group CRO; Risk Director (smaller building societies) `[unverified]`.

OWNS:
- the RMF and RAF design;
- appetite calibration and recommendations;
- the board and BRC risk pack;
- second-line challenge;
- the model risk framework (SS1/23 where applicable);
- the reportability call with SMF16 and SMF17 where the matter is prudential.

REPORTS_TO: the CEO, with independent access to the BRC chair (Leeds example, M-E-02, M-J-06).

SECONDARY_PERSONAS:
- **BRC chair (SMF10):** the board consumer, sponsor and gatekeeper;
- **Head of Compliance (SMF16);**
- **Head of Financial Crime / MLRO (SMF17);**
- **CFO (SMF2):** capital, planning and ICAAP;
- **first-line risk owners;**
- **Head of Internal Audit (SMF5).**

ROUTED_EXECS:
- the board and the BRC (decisions on appetite, waivers and tensions);
- the CEO and ExCo (growth versus capacity);
- the CFO (capital and planning);
- SMF16 and SMF17 (reportability, financial crime);
- first-line owners (remediation);
- the COO and CTO (feeds, change, resilience).

## Decision cadence and day-in-the-life

DECISION_CADENCE:
- **Monthly:** ERC pack.
- **Quarterly:** BRC, plus the board risk report.
- **Annual:** RAS refresh with planning, ICAAP and ILAAP, and the Consumer Duty board report input.
- **Event-driven:** breaches, incidents and reportability calls, which run on "immediately" clocks.

Time-travel default: quarter-on-quarter, with period-over-period change shown (CF-003).

DAILY_JOB `[unverified -- confirm in CRO interview]`:
- **What they open the tool for:**
  - to see what has moved against appetite since the last committee, and what is heading for a threshold;
  - to check that what they will tell the board is defensible: owned, reconciled and attested;
  - to see which decisions the board must take (waivers, trade-offs, appetite changes) and what may be reportable.
- **What they do not do:** work control-level queues. That is the SMF16 CRSA cockpit and the first line.

## KPI vocabulary (grounded)

NORTH-STAR:
- appetite utilisation and headroom per principal risk;
- metrics outside appetite (count and persistence);
- capital and liquidity against internal limits (CET1, leverage, LCR, NSFR).

Sources: M-A-08, M-A-10, M-B-03 to M-B-17.

DIAGNOSTIC:
- leading-indicator drift and time to threshold;
- cost of risk and 3m+ arrears;
- impact-tolerance headroom;
- VREQ / s166 adherence;
- chain integrity (owned, reconciled, attested, mapped);
- overdue audit and regulatory actions;
- days from awareness to notification;
- escalation latency;
- the self-identification ratio;
- Consumer Duty outcome tolerances.

Sources: M-A-13, M-C-08, M-E-03, M-F-01.

## Must-show components (with why)
- **Strategy strip with appetite consumed.** Risk as a strategic lever is the redesign thesis (M-A-09, M-A-10).
- **RAS headline scorecard.** A configurable cap (default about 12) over full Level 1 coverage, because no rule sets a number (M-A-08).
- **Headroom gauge** (set vs actual vs forecast, with a denominator). The board needs position, not just colour (M-A-07, M-S-01).
- **Chain-integrity badge** on every headline. This is what lets the CRO defend a figure (M-D-06, M-H-04).
- **Restriction adherence test** for VREQs and s166 actions. This is the anchor enforcement failure (M-H-02, M-H-03).
- **Exception feed:** Level 1 reds and ambers, persistent Level 2 ambers, and unremediated items past SLA. This prevents suppression (M-B-19, M-B-20).
- **Tension register summary.** Governs deliberate trade-offs (M-G-04).
- **Reportability ledger (summary).** Many clocks, with a human decision (M-C-12, M-C-13).
- **Top and emerging risk overlay** mapped to principal risks (M-B-03, M-B-10).

## Remove for this role (with why)
- **Control-level RCSA lists and reviewer queues.** These belong to SMF16 and the first line. The CRO view is one level up (the reviewer's core critique; M-A-02).
- **Matrices, heat-maps and bubble maps.** These are auto-rejected at CRO and exec altitude (RP-001).
- **Raw compliance miss-counts.** The CRO sees developing risk by category (RP-008).
- **Wide AI bars.** Use a compact sparkle card instead (RP-006).
- **A cost or ROI lens on financial crime controls.** Ethics position: it reproduces the NatWest failure shape.

## Trust threshold / hard fails
**Trust threshold.** Before the CRO will present a number, it must carry:
- the threshold and its rationale;
- a trajectory;
- a named SMF and risk owner;
- the date of the last reconciliation;
- the attestation status;
- a drill to evidence in one click.

**Hard fails:**
- "within appetite" on an unreconciled feed;
- a headroom or coverage figure with no denominator;
- a restriction shown as compliant because a flag fired, rather than because a population test passed;
- an AI verdict with no reason, or AI shown deciding reportability;
- practice labelled as rule.

## UI expectation and theme
- **Layout:** C-suite altitude: least detail, most status (CL-012). Business-question card titles (CL-001). Headline top-left, with deltas beside the number. One-click drill maximum (LR-007). A thin AI executive-summary band (CL-004).
- **Default comparison:** quarter-on-quarter.
- **Density:** low to medium.

## Handoffs
- **Down:** to SMF16 (CRSA cockpit), to SMF17 (Head of Financial Crime workspace) and to first-line owners (remediation).
- **Up:** to the BRC and the board. The board view is the CRO's pack to the BRC.
- **Sideways:** to the CFO for capital and planning.

## Common build mistakes for this persona
- The control-level view at board altitude (the original critique).
- A wall of green.
- Lenses as tabs the board must learn. Earlier advisory feedback was to move Ownership to the top level and split the strategic tier from the operational tier.
- Labels that need explaining.
- A hard-coded 10–12 cap.
- Fraud as a lens rather than a filter.

## Example primary screen set
1. **Board Risk View:** strategy strip, RAS scorecard with headroom and trajectory, exception feed, chain-integrity summary, top and emerging overlay.
2. **Principal-risk drill:** threshold cascade, leading-indicator tree, owners, tension pairs, precedent link.
3. **Decisions and reportability:** appetite waivers and tension decisions due at the BRC, plus the reportability ledger with its clocks and human decisions.
4. *(optional)* **Restriction adherence:** VREQ / s166 population tests with denominators.
