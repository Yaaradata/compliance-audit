### 1.5 Trust threshold (per-persona hard fail, which feeds the 9C AUTO_REJECT)

Before Eleanor will present a number to the board, the card must carry all of the following:

1. **Position with a denominator.** Appetite set against actual (and forecast where relevant), in the metric's own unit and in £ where it applies. Headroom with no denominator is a hard fail.
2. **Threshold and rationale.** The cascade (capacity → red → amber → operating limit) and its approved calibration rationale, one click away.
3. **Trajectory.** A rule-based least-squares projection labelled "early warning, not a prediction".
4. **A named SMF and risk owner.** The role is on the face; the name is in the drill.
5. **Last reconciliation date and attestation status.** This is the four-dot chain-integrity badge (owner · feed · attestation · mapping). It is always present and quiet, and turns red as "unverified" on failure (D8 and P7 recommendation; confirm at Stage 6).
6. **A recommended action and who decides.** A "prepare" or "propose" draft with its human gate, plus the routed exec.
7. **One-click drill to evidence** (LR-007). There is no third layer.
8. **For AI elements:** the AI marker, a one-line plain-language reason, and "accepted by [name] on [date]" once accepted.
9. **For Tier 2 partial cards:** the "Can tell / cannot tell" line on the drill, using the fixed copy "no action, decision or escalation recorded" (P2).
10. **For synthetic data:** `[illustrative]`. Precedents are real and posture-tagged.

**Hard fails.** At 9C each becomes an AUTO_REJECT candidate; at Stage 11 each becomes a HIGH finding.

## 4. Routing map (who routes what to whom)

The AI drafts or prepares, a named human gates, and every hop below is audit-logged. Labels are "prepare", "propose" or "send to owner", never "file" or "notify". Format: **from --(what, card)--> to [human gate]**.

### 4.1 Upward (CRO → BRC, board, CEO)

```
CRO --(appetite recommendation: re-phase / change appetite / confirm headroom use; T2-1, T2-2)--> BRC chair --> board   [board decides; CFO signs off capital figures]
CRO --(capacity proposal: restore capacity OR pace growth; never "lower the standard"; T2-5)--> CEO and ExCo --> board   [CEO and board decide]
CRO --(trade-off re-decision paper; T2-6) and (waiver renewal or restoration; T2-7)--> BRC chair --> board   [board decides; entry keyed into the decision register, D1]
CRO --(exception feed, cannot be suppressed: T2-34, T2-36, T2-38; override log T2-35)--> BRC chair   [BRC chair sets the agenda; any override needs a named approver and reason code]
CRO --(escalation latency; governance review proposal; T2-37)--> BRC chair   [BRC chair commissions a review]
CRO --(S1 out-of-cycle call: T2-5 S1, T2-14 S1, T2-15, T2-27)--> BRC chair   [at the CRO's discretion (P8); never automatic]
CRO --(reportability ledger counts by status; T2-29)--> board   [summary only; no SAR content; T2-30 never goes to the BRC or board (D6)]
CRO --(SMF reassignment proposal; MRM change draft; T2-16)--> CEO --> company secretary   [CEO approves]
```

### 4.2 Sideways (CRO ↔ peer SMFs and the CFO)

