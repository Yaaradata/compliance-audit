Glossary
RASRisk appetite statement: the board's limits for each risk it takes.
Level 0Obligatory risks with zero appetite: regulatory must-haves, never traded off.
Top risksTier-one risks the board steers directly, with tight bands.
Emerging risksForward-looking risks with wider bands, still being measured.
Obligatory risksRegulatory duties: the Level 0 tier.
Amber triggerThe early-warning point before the appetite limit.
Appetite limitThe line the board has said it will not cross.
Leading / laggingLeading measures warn early; lagging measures confirm what happened.
LCRLiquidity coverage ratio: cash-like assets against 30 days of stressed outflows.
NSFRNet stable funding ratio: stable funding against longer-term needs.
CET1Common equity tier 1: the core capital ratio.
High-LTVMortgages with a high loan-to-value, here for first-time buyers.
LTILoan-to-income: loan size as a multiple of income, e.g. above 4.5×.
EPCEnergy performance certificate: home efficiency bands A to G.
SMFSenior manager function: a named regulated role with personal accountability.
SARSuspicious activity report: a report to the National Crime Agency.
Consumer DutyThe FCA (Financial Conduct Authority) rule on good customer outcomes.
Read-acrossChecking whether one event points to the same gap elsewhere.
AttestationA risk owner's monthly sign-off that their figures and controls are right.
Line of sightWhether a named owner can see a customer journey end to end.
First / second / third lineBusiness owns risk; risk and compliance challenge; internal audit assures.
BacktestTesting a change on historical data before using it live.

READING THE BOARD VIEW IN TWO MINUTES

  1. Read the headline: the main problem and the ask.
  2. Check the four objectives: on track, at risk or off track.
  3. Look at what is outside appetite, worst first.
  4. Note what may need telling to the regulator, and who decides.
  5. Take the board decisions: tighten, hold or loosen.
  6. Check who owns each risk and whether they have signed this month.
